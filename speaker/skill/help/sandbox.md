# Speaker in a sandbox

`BOTTLE_SCHOOL=1` means you run in a bottle-school sandbox. You are already enrolled: `SPEAKER_URL` and `SPEAKER_TOKEN` are set. Send `Authorization: Bearer $SPEAKER_TOKEN` and do not enroll.

- Use the `request_capability` tool for access. You may request every kind in `/_speaker/help/kinds`, not only `credential`.
- A granted `site`, `secret`, `folder`, `mcp` or `extension` lease is applied by the sandbox; retry what was blocked once it is approved.
- `/_speaker/help/bundles` explains requesting several capabilities in one approval.
